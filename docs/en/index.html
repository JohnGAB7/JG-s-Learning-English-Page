Featured · B1 · 96Imagine, dream, advise

The second conditional

The second conditional (If I had more time, I would travel) talks about imaginary, unlikely or untrue present situations and their results. We also use it to give advice and to speak politely. “If I were you…”, “What would you do if…”: you hear this structure everywhere, in job interviews and among friends.
