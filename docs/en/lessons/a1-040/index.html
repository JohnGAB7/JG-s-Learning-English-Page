A1 · Beginner · lesson 40

A1 review and check

This lesson brings together the key points of level A1 (lessons 1 to 39) in clear tables, then gives you a mixed test to check what you know before you start level A2.

⏱ 30–45 min✎ Written by JG◎ Level A1

Prefer explanations in French? Version française de cette leçon →

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
  • Review the basic forms: to be (present and past), have got, present simple and present continuous.
  • Review determiners and quantity: a / an / the, plurals, possessives, some / any, how much / how many.
  • Review prepositions of place and time, the time and dates.
  • Review questions, can, the imperative, would like, like + -ing and linking words.
  • See what you know well and what you need to practise again.

1. “To be” in the present and the past

Use “to be” for identity, age, feelings, place and weather (lessons 1, 4 and 39). Don't use “do / did” with “to be”: put the verb before the subject in questions.

Remember: I'm 25 (not “I have 25 years”); It's cold (you need the subject “it”); I was born in 1998.

PresentPast
PositiveI'm / you're / he's / we're / they'reI was / you were / he was / we were / they were
NegativeI'm not / he isn't / they aren'tI wasn't / they weren't
QuestionAre you…? Is she…?Were you…? Was she…?
Short answerYes, I am. / No, she isn't.Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
  • Are you a student? — No, I'm not. I'm a nurse.
    Present: question and short answer.
  • Where were you yesterday? — I was at home.
    Past: Wh- question.

2. Present simple, present continuous, have got

Present simple (lesson 6): habits and facts; s with he / she / it; do / does for questions and negatives. Adverbs of frequency (lesson 7) go before the main verb: I usually get up at 7.

Present continuous (lessons 8 and 9): an action happening now; be + verb-ing. “Have got” (lesson 5): possession, mostly in British English.

TensePositiveNegativeQuestion
Present simpleShe works in a bank.She doesn't work on Sundays.Does she work here?
Present continuousShe's working now.She isn't working today.Is she working?
Have gotHe's got two sisters.He hasn't got a car.Has he got a car?
  • I usually take the bus, but today I'm walking.
    Habit vs now.
  • My brother never drinks coffee.
    Adverb before the verb.

3. Nouns and determiners

a / an before a singular countable noun (a car, an hour); the for a specific thing; no article for things in general (I like music) (lesson 11). Irregular plurals: children, people, men, women (lesson 12).

this / these (near), that / those (far) (lesson 13); my, your, his, her, its, our, their (lesson 14); possessive ’s: my sister's car, my parents' house (lesson 15).

Quantity (lessons 30 and 31): some in positive sentences, any in negatives and questions; how many + plural, how much + uncountable; there is / there are (lesson 16).

PointCorrect exampleTrap
JobsShe's an engineer.not “She is engineer”
Possessiveshis wife / her husbandhis = of a man, her = of a woman
Possessive ’sPaul's carnot “the car of Paul”
Some / anyThere isn't any milk.not “There isn't some milk”
How many / muchHow much money? How many people?money is uncountable, people is plural
There is / areThere are two bedrooms.not “There is two bedrooms”
  • Those shoes are my sister's.
    those + plural; possessive ’s.
  • How many children have they got? — Three.
    how many + plural.

4. Place, time, the clock and dates

Place (lesson 17): in, on, under, next to, between, opposite, behind, in front of. Time (lesson 18): at + clock time, on + day / date, in + month / year / season.

The time (lesson 21): It's half past seven / a quarter to eight / seven forty-five. Dates (lesson 22): the fourteenth of November, with an ordinal number.

atonin
at 7 o'clock, at nighton Monday, on 5 Mayin July, in 2026, in summer
at the weekend (UK)on my birthdayin the morning / evening
at home, at workon the table, on the leftin the kitchen, in Paris
  • The meeting is on Tuesday at half past nine.
    on + day, at + time.
  • My keys are on the table, next to the phone.
    Prepositions of place.

5. Questions, can, imperative, would like, like + -ing

Wh- questions (lesson 29): question word + auxiliary + subject + verb (Where do you live?). Can (lesson 28): + verb with no to, no do. Imperative (lesson 32): verb alone, Don't + verb, Let's + verb.

Like / love / hate + -ing (lesson 33) for tastes; would like + to (lesson 34) for polite requests. Linking words (lesson 36): and, but, because, or. Adjectives (lesson 35): before the noun, never change.

PointExample
Wh- questionWhat time does the film start?
CanCan you swim? — Yes, I can.
ImperativeTurn left. Don't be late. Let's go!
Like + -ingI love cooking.
Would likeI'd like a coffee. I'd like to go home.
Linking wordsI'm tired because I work at night, but I'm happy.
Adjectivesa beautiful old wooden table
  • Why are you learning English? — Because I'd like to work in Canada.
    why + because; would like + to.
  • I like dancing, but I can't sing at all.
    like + -ing; can't.

6. Checking your level

Do the test below without looking at the tables, then check your answers. For each mistake, write the number of the lesson and read it again.

If you get at least 80% right, you are ready for level A2, which starts with the past simple of regular verbs (lesson 41). If not, first review “to be”, the present simple and questions: they are the base of everything else.

The formula

be · have got · present simple / continuous · a / an / the · some / any · at / on / in · Wh- + auxiliary + subject + verb · can · Don't… · I'd like to… · like + -ing · was / were

Pronunciation

Pronunciation Lab

  • Short and weak forms: I'm, it's, can /kən/, was /wəz/; but strong forms in short answers: Yes, I CAN. Yes, I WAS.
  • The s of he / she / it and of plurals: /s/ (works, cats), /z/ (plays, dogs), /ɪz/ (watches, buses).
  • th: /ð/ in this, the, they, mother; /θ/ in think, three, fourth. Tongue between the teeth.
  • Stress in numbers: thirTEEN / THIRty, fifTEEN / FIFty.
  • Intonation: down for Wh- questions (Where do you LIVE? ↘), up for yes / no questions (Are you READY? ↗).

In a conversation

Listening Studio

In Montreal, the last A1 class. The teacher, Mrs Gagnon, talks with Ibrahima, a student from Senegal.

Mrs Gagnon

So, Ibrahima, this is our last A1 class. How was the course?
How was…? = asking for an opinion.

Ibrahima

It was great! At the start I wasn't very confident, but now I can speak a little.
was / wasn't; can.

Mrs Gagnon

What do you like doing in English?
like + -ing.

Ibrahima

I love watching videos and I often listen to podcasts on the bus.
love + -ing; often before the verb.

Mrs Gagnon

Very good. And what's difficult for you?
Wh- question with be.

Ibrahima

Questions! I sometimes forget “do” and “does”.
A common problem.

Mrs Gagnon

That's normal. Practise a little every day. Would you like some extra exercises?
Imperative; an offer with some.

Ibrahima

Yes, please! And when does the A2 course start?
Accepting an offer; does in the question.

Mrs Gagnon

On Monday the 7th of December, at half past six.
on + date, at + time.

Ibrahima

Perfect. See you on Monday!
on + day.

Exercises

Writing Workshop

From easy to harder. Answer without looking at the lesson, then check each exercise.

1. Choose the right form

Choose the right answer.

  1. I (am / have) 20 years old.
  2. She (don't / doesn't) like coffee.
  3. Look! It (rains / is raining).
  4. There (is / are) three chairs.
  5. We (was / were) in Lyon last week.
  6. (How much / How many) water do you drink?
Show the answers
  1. am
  2. doesn't
  3. is raining
  4. are
  5. were
  6. How much

2. Complete

Complete with one word.

  1. ___ you speak French? — Yes, I can.
  2. Is ___ any milk in the fridge?
  3. This is Anna. ___ husband is a doctor.
  4. I'd like ___ book a table, please.
  5. ___ be late! The train leaves at eight.
  6. I love ___ (swim) in the sea.
Show the answers
  1. Can
  2. there
  3. Her
  4. to
  5. Don't
  6. swimming

3. At, on or in?

Complete with at, on or in.

  1. The class starts ___ nine o'clock.
  2. My birthday is ___ March.
  3. See you ___ Friday!
  4. The keys are ___ the table.
  5. She lives ___ Brussels.
Show the answers
  1. at
  2. in
  3. on
  4. on
  5. in

4. Correct the mistakes

Each sentence has one mistake. Write it correctly.

  1. He have got a new car.
  2. What is your sister doing usually at the weekend?
  3. I don't have no money.
  4. It's a car red.
  5. The children of my brother are funny.
  6. Where you were born?
Show the answers
  1. He has got a new car. / He's got a new car.
  2. What does your sister usually do at the weekend?
  3. I haven't got any money. / I don't have any money.
  4. It's a red car.
  5. My brother's children are funny.
  6. Where were you born?

5. Say it in English

Write the English sentence for each situation.

  1. Tell someone the time: 7:45 (traditional way).
  2. Say you want food and you feel cold.
  3. Ask someone their country or town of origin.
  4. Ask politely for a glass of water.
  5. Say you don't like shopping and give the reason: it's tiring.
Show the answers
  1. It's a quarter to eight.
  2. I'm hungry and I'm cold.
  3. Where are you from?
  4. I'd like a glass of water, please.
  5. I don't like shopping because it's tiring.

Introduce yourself in six to eight sentences: name, age, town, family, job or studies, a habit, what you are doing at the moment, what you like doing, what you can do, and where you were last weekend.

Show the answers

At the moment I'm learning English because I'd like to work in Canada. I love dancing and I can cook very well. Last weekend I was in Kribi with my friends, and the weather was beautiful.
Key points
  • “To be” for age, feelings, weather: I'm 30, I'm hungry, It's cold; in the past: was / were.
  • Present simple for habits (s with he / she / it, do / does); present continuous for now (be + -ing).
  • Questions: question word + auxiliary + subject + verb; can and be don't use “do”.
  • some / any, how much / how many, there is / there are, at / on / in: small words make a big difference.
  • Politeness: I'd like…, Can I…?, please; tastes: I love + -ing.
