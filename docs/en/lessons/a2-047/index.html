Superlatives

We use superlatives to show that one thing is above all the others in a group: “the biggest”, “the most expensive”, “the least interesting”. They work like comparatives: -est for short words, most for long words.

⏱ 30–45 min✎ Written by JG◎ Level A2

Prefer explanations in French? Version française de cette leçon →

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
  • Form superlatives of short adjectives (the -est) and long adjectives (the most).
  • Know the irregular superlatives: the best, the worst, the furthest.
  • Use the right preposition after a superlative: in for a place or group, of for a period or a list.
  • Use “one of the + superlative + plural noun” and “the least”.

Short adjectives: the + -est

For one-syllable adjectives and two-syllable adjectives ending in -y, add -est and put “the” before it. The spelling rules are the same as for comparatives (lesson 46).

We almost always use “the”, because we are talking about one thing only.

RuleAdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
+ -esttalltallerthe tallest
ends in -e: + -stnicenicerthe nicest
1 vowel + 1 consonant: double itbigbiggerthe biggest
consonant + -y: y → iesthappyhappierthe happiest
ends in -w or vowel + -y: no doublingnewnewerthe newest
  • Mount Cameroon is the highest mountain in Cameroon.
    high → the highest.
  • Sunday is the busiest day at the market.
    busy → the busiest.

Long adjectives: the most + adjective

For two-syllable adjectives that do not end in -y, and for adjectives with three or more syllables, use “the most” + adjective.

For the opposite idea, use “the least” + adjective, with any adjective: the least expensive, the least difficult.

  • This is the most beautiful beach in Senegal.
    beautiful: long → the most.
  • Health is the most important thing.
    important: long → the most.
  • It was the least interesting film of the festival.
    the least = the opposite of the most.

Irregular superlatives

Adjectives that are irregular in the comparative are also irregular in the superlative. They are some of the most common words in English.

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlativeExample
goodbetterthe bestShe's the best student in the class.
badworsethe worstThat was the worst day of my life.
farfurther / fartherthe furthest / the farthestWhich planet is the furthest from the sun?
much / manymorethe mostWho has the most money?
littlelessthe leastHe did the least work.

In or of after a superlative?

Use “in” before a place or a group seen as one unit: in the world, in my family, in the class, in the team. Do not say “the biggest city of the country”.

Use “of” before a period of time or a list of things: of the year, of my life, of all, of the three.

  • He's the tallest boy in his class.
    in + group.
  • It's the biggest city in Canada.
    in + place.
  • It was the happiest day of my life.
    of + period.
  • She's the youngest of the three sisters.
    of + list.

Useful patterns

“One of the + superlative + plural noun”: the noun is always plural, because you choose from many.

With a possessive, there is no “the”: my best friend, our biggest problem.

Superlative + present perfect + ever: “the best film I've ever seen”. You will see this again with ever / never (lesson 52).

  • Brussels is one of the most expensive cities in Belgium.
    Plural noun: cities.
  • My best friend lives in Dakar.
    No the with my.
  • It's the best meal I've ever had.
    Superlative + I've ever + past participle.

The formula

the + short adjective-est · the most / the least + long adjective · (in + place / group; of + period / list)

Pronunciation

Pronunciation Lab

  • The -est ending is /ɪst/: biggest /ˈbɪɡɪst/, nicest /ˈnaɪsɪst/, happiest /ˈhæpiɪst/.
  • “the” is /ðə/ before a consonant sound and /ði/ before a vowel sound: the biggest /ðə/, the oldest /ði ˈəʊldɪst/.
  • “most” is /məʊst/ (UK) or /moʊst/ (US).
  • In “the best”, the final t is light and often links to the next word: the best actor /ðə ˈbest ˈæktə/.
  • Stress the adjective: the MOST imPORtant, the BIGgest.

Common mistakes

Don’t saySayWhy
✗ She is the most tall in the family.✓ She is the tallest in the family.Short adjective: -est, not most.
✗ It's the most biggest city.✓ It's the biggest city.Never most + -est.
✗ He's the better player of the team.✓ He's the best player in the team.good → the best; and in + group.
✗ It's the oldest building of the city.✓ It's the oldest building in the city.Place → in, not of.
✗ One of the best restaurant.✓ One of the best restaurants.One of the … + plural noun.
✗ She's my the best friend.✓ She's my best friend.No the after a possessive.
✗ It was the baddest moment.✓ It was the worst moment.bad → the worst.

In a conversation

Listening Studio

Two colleagues, Sami and Julie, are planning a weekend in Bruges and reading reviews online.

Julie

Which hotel is the cheapest?
cheap → the cheapest.

Sami

The Canal Inn. But it's also the furthest from the centre.
far → the furthest.

Julie

What about the Belfry Hotel?
“What about…?” asks about another option.

Sami

It's the most expensive, but it has the best reviews in town.
the most + long adjective; good → the best.

Julie

What's the most famous thing to see in Bruges?
the most famous.

Sami

The Belfry, I think — the medieval tower in the main square. It's one of the most popular sights in the city.
one of the + superlative + plural noun.

Julie

And the best chocolate shop?
the best.

Sami

That's the most difficult question of the day!
of + period (the day).

  2. big
  3. funny
  4. expensive
  5. good
  6. bad
  7. safe
  8. interesting

  2. the biggest
  3. the funniest

2. In or of?

Complete with in or of.

  1. It's the tallest building ___ Africa.
  2. It was the best day ___ the holiday.
  3. She's the oldest ___ my four cousins.
  4. He's the fastest runner ___ the team.
  5. It's the coldest month ___ the year.
Show the answers
  1. in
  2. of
  3. of
  4. in

3. Complete

Put the adjective in brackets in the superlative.

  1. Toronto is ___ (large) city in Canada.
  2. That was ___ (bad) film of the year.
  3. Lagos is one of ___ (crowded) cities in the world.
  4. It's ___ (easy) exercise in the book.
  5. This is ___ (little) expensive option.
Show the answers
  1. the largest
  2. the worst
  3. the most crowded
  4. the easiest
  5. the least

4. Correct the mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Write it correctly.

  1. It's the most cheap phone.
  2. She's the better singer in the group.
  3. Paris is one of the most beautiful city.
  4. He's the richest man of the country.

  1. It's the cheapest phone.
  2. She's the best singer in the group.
  3. Paris is one of the most beautiful cities.
  4. He's the richest man in the country.

5. Write the sentence

Use the words to write a sentence with a superlative.

  1. it / good / restaurant / the town
  2. it / be (past) / bad / day / my life
  3. it / one of / hot / countries / the world
Show the answers
  1. It's the best restaurant in the town.
  2. It was the worst day of my life.
  3. It's one of the hottest countries in the world.

6. Your turn

Present your town or country in five sentences with superlatives (the biggest, the most famous, the best…).

  1. Model answer
Show the answers
  1. I live in Liège. It's one of the biggest cities in Wallonia. The most famous place is the Montagne de Bueren, a staircase with 374 steps. The best time to visit is in summer. The busiest day is Sunday, when there is a huge market by the river.
Key points
  • Short adjective: the + -est (the cheapest); long adjective: the most + adjective.
  • Irregular: the best, the worst, the furthest; the opposite: the least.
  • in + place or group (in the world, in my class); of + period or list (of the year, of the three).
  • One of the + superlative + plural noun.
  • With a possessive, no the: my best friend.
