A1 · CEFR · Beginner · lessons 1 to 40

Build your foundations.

Understand and use familiar everyday expressions. Introduce yourself, ask simple questions and interact in predictable situations.

Grammar

Pronouns, be / have, articles, plurals, present simple, basic questions, demonstratives, possessives, can, there is / are, simple prepositions.

Vocabulary

Identity, the alphabet, numbers, dates, countries, family, home, objects, food, time, routines and places.

Vocabulary & Expressions

Pronunciation

The alphabet and spelling, key sounds, word stress, sentence rhythm and question intonation.

Pronunciation Lab

Listening

Short dialogues at a slow pace, instructions, personal information and very simple conversations.

Listening Studio

Speaking

Introduce yourself, talk about what you like, describe a person or a place, answer simple questions.

Reading and writing

Forms, signs, short messages, simple descriptions, personal sentences and short notes.

Reading Room

Practical English

Greet, thank, ask someone to repeat, spell, ask a price, order simply, ask for directions.

A2 · CEFR · Elementary · lessons 41 to 90

Become more independent.

Handle routine exchanges and describe your surroundings, needs, activities and past events.

Grammar

Past simple, past continuous, future plans, quantifiers, comparatives and superlatives, frequency, modals, a first look at the present perfect, infinitive and gerund.

Vocabulary

Work, studies, shopping, transport, health, travel, meals, free time, weather, services and relationships.

Vocabulary & Expressions

Pronunciation

-ed endings and plurals, contractions, weak forms, common linking and intonation.

Pronunciation Lab

Listening

Everyday conversations, announcements, instructions, exchanges at work and in shops.

Listening Studio

Speaking

Describe your habits, tell a recent story, explain your preferences, organise an activity and ask for help.

Reading and writing

Simple emails, instructions, short articles, linked paragraphs, invitations, requests and descriptions.

Writing Workshop

Practical English

Make an appointment, order, buy, explain a problem, suggest, accept, refuse and make a polite request.

B1 · CEFR · Intermediate · lessons 91 to 150

Speak with confidence.

Be independent on familiar topics, tell about experiences, explain choices and give a reasoned opinion.

Grammar

Tense contrasts, narrative tenses, future forms, conditionals, relative clauses, passive, reported speech, modals of deduction, gerund and infinitive.

Vocabulary

Education, careers, society, technology, the environment, culture, travel, relationships, emotions and personal growth.

Vocabulary & Expressions

Pronunciation

Thought groups, sentence stress, linking, weak forms, rhythm and intelligibility.

Pronunciation Lab

Listening

Interviews, longer conversations, explanations, accessible podcasts and standard speech.

Listening Studio

Speaking

Tell a story, explain a process, compare, justify, give an opinion and keep a longer conversation going.

Reading and writing

Articles, opinion pieces, stories, structured emails, argued paragraphs and short reports.

Reading Room

Practical English

Clarify, rephrase, sort out a misunderstanding, make a suggestion, negotiate a solution and share an experience.

B2 · CEFR · Upper intermediate · lessons 151 to 220

Express, debate & nuance.

Understand complex content, defend a point of view and communicate spontaneously and fluently.

Grammar

Advanced tense contrasts, all conditionals, passive, reported speech, nuanced modals, participle clauses, inversion and emphasis.

Vocabulary

Collocations, phrasal verbs, idioms in context, abstract, professional and academic vocabulary.

Vocabulary & Expressions

Pronunciation

Expressive intonation, emphasis, pace, nuance and adapting to the listener.

Pronunciation Lab

Listening

Long authentic recordings, a range of accents, implied meaning, interviews and discussions.

Listening Studio

Speaking

Argue, negotiate, hypothesise, summarise, compare points of view and debate spontaneously.

Reading and writing

Complex articles, essays, reports, proposals, formal letters and summaries.

Writing Workshop

Practical English

Disagree with nuance, persuade, defend a recommendation, manage a discussion and adapt your register.

C1 · CEFR · Advanced · lessons 221 to 290

Master precision & flexibility.

Understand demanding texts and express yourself spontaneously with precision, flexibility and control of register.

Grammar and discourse

Complex structures, inversion, ellipsis, cleft sentences, hedging, modality, register, discourse markers and fine nuances of tense and aspect.

Vocabulary and style

Lexical precision, idiomatic language, connotation, metaphor, register, and academic and professional phrasing.

Vocabulary & Expressions

Pronunciation

Expressive control, rhythm, discourse intonation and clarity in varied contexts.

Pronunciation Lab

Listening

Implied attitudes, fast speech, varied accents, lectures and complex discussions.

Listening Studio

Speaking

Nuanced argument, presentations, facilitation, diplomacy, complex storytelling and spontaneous adaptation.

Reading and writing

Long demanding texts, rhetoric, synthesis, critical analysis, proposals and nuanced professional writing.

Writing Workshop

Practical English

Handle ambiguity, persuade with tact, rephrase precisely, adjust your tone and catch what is implied.
