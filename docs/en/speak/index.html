Speak English in Real Life

Don’t just learn English. Live it.

Learn to talk about your real life: your habits, your tastes, your work, your plans, your relationships and your ideas. Listen, watch, imitate, repeat, then answer in your own words.

The method

Listen. Imitate. Make it yours.

Each topic is a short speaking course. The video is a living model: rhythm, pronunciation, natural expressions and possible answers.

01

Watch & understand

Watch a short scene with subtitles. Understand the situation, the intention and the useful expressions.

Listening Studio
02

Listen & imitate

Listen sentence by sentence. Notice the stress, linking, rhythm and intonation, then imitate out loud.

Pronunciation Lab
03

Repeat & rebuild

Repeat with pauses, then replace the details with your own: your time, your tastes, your job, your experience.

Memory & Review
04

Answer naturally

Answer a question without reading. Compare your answer with the model and try again, more fluently.

Progression

Same topic, richer language.

Each topic comes back at every level, with more ambitious goals.

A1–A2

Name, describe, answer in simple sentences and talk about your habits.

B1

Tell, explain, compare and hold a conversation for several minutes.

B2

Develop a point of view, add nuance, argue and interact spontaneously.

C1

Express yourself with precision, naturalness, subtlety and awareness of context.
