The project

A library, not an inbox.

The English lessons of the “Grandir chaque jour” newsletter were getting lost in emails. Here, every lesson has its own page, always available; the email keeps the exercises, the answers and the reminders.

01

A guided path

The content follows the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR), from A1 to C1, and keeps the numbers of the lessons already sent.

02

Your language, your choice

This English version explains everything in English. The French version (FR button) explains the same lessons in French.

03

Remember for the long term

Each lesson comes back 3, 7 and 30 days later; the review cards focus on what you miss.

Our commitments

  • Accuracy. Every word links to the Cambridge Dictionary; every news item links to its original article.
  • Transparency. The content is written with the help of Claude (AI) and reviewed. Found a mistake? Reply to the newsletter and it will be corrected.
  • Privacy. No cookies, no subscriber data. Only your theme, your news tab and your review progress are saved, in your own browser.
  • Free. The lessons are free, and so are the recommended tools.
