As … as et la comparaison d’égalité

Après les comparatifs (leçon 46) et les superlatifs (leçon 47), voici la comparaison d'égalité : « aussi … que » se dit as … as. À la forme négative, not as … as signifie « pas aussi … que », c'est-à-dire « moins … que ». La leçon présente aussi the same as et as much / as many as, ainsi que les pièges typiques des francophones (« as … than », « same than »).

⏱ 30–45 min✎ Rédigé par JG◎ Niveau A2

Envie d’immersion ? This lesson in English →

À la fin de cette leçon, tu sauras :
  • Former as + adjectif / adverbe + as pour dire « aussi … que ».
  • Utiliser not as … as (ou not so … as) pour dire « moins … que » de façon naturelle.
  • Employer the same (as), as much / as many … as et similar to / different from.
  • Choisir le bon pronom après as (as tall as me / as tall as I am).

La forme : as + adjectif + as

Pour dire que deux choses sont égales, on met l'adjectif à sa forme simple entre deux as : Tom is as tall as his father. (Tom est aussi grand que son père.)

L'adjectif ne change jamais : pas de -er, pas de more. On dit « as big as », jamais « as bigger as » ni « as more interesting as ».

Le deuxième as remplace « que » : ne mets pas than. « As … than » est une erreur très fréquente.

SensStructureExemple
aussi … queas + adjectif + asLyon is as busy as Marseille.
aussi … que (adverbe)as + adverbe + asShe runs as fast as her brother.
pas aussi … quenot as + adjectif + asMy flat isn't as big as yours.
presque aussi … quealmost / nearly as … asIt's nearly as hot as yesterday.
deux fois plus … quetwice as … asThis bag is twice as heavy as mine.
  • The film is as good as the book.
    Égalité.
  • Kribi is as beautiful as I imagined.
    as + phrase complète.
  • Can you speak as slowly as possible?
    as … as possible = le plus … possible.

La forme négative : not as … as

Not as … as signifie « pas aussi … que », donc « moins … que ». C'est la façon la plus naturelle de dire « moins » en anglais : My car isn't as fast as yours est plus courant que « My car is less fast than yours ».

On entend aussi not so … as, avec le même sens, mais not as … as est plus fréquent.

Attention au sens : The test wasn't as difficult as last year = il était PLUS facile que l'année dernière.

  • Today isn't as cold as yesterday.
    = Today is warmer than yesterday.
  • I'm not as good at maths as my sister.
    Elle est meilleure que moi.
  • The bus isn't as expensive as the taxi.
    Le bus coûte moins cher.

Après as : me ou I am ?

Après le deuxième as, on peut mettre un nom, un pronom complément ou une proposition : as tall as Paul / as tall as me / as tall as I am.

À l'oral, le pronom complément (me, him, her, us, them) est le plus courant : She's as old as me. « As old as I » seul est très formel et sonne étrange ; si on veut le pronom sujet, on ajoute le verbe : as old as I am.

On peut aussi comparer deux moments : The town is as quiet as it was ten years ago.

  • My brother is as tall as me.
    Pronom complément (courant).
  • My brother is as tall as I am.
    Pronom sujet + verbe (soigné).
  • He doesn't work as hard as he did before.
    Comparaison entre deux moments.

As much / as many … as

Pour comparer des quantités : as much + indénombrable + as, as many + dénombrable pluriel + as (voir la leçon 58). I don't earn as much money as you. She has as many books as a library!

Sans nom, après un verbe : He eats as much as his father. (Il mange autant que son père.)

Attention : en français on dit « autant de … que », mais en anglais il n'y a pas de « of » : as many friends as, pas « as many of friends as ».

  • There aren't as many tourists as in August.
    many + dénombrable.
  • I don't drink as much coffee as before.
    much + indénombrable.
  • You can take as many photos as you want.
    Autant que tu veux.

The same as, similar to, different from

The same (le même, la même) : My phone is the same as yours. Avec un nom : We're in the same class. Attention : toujours the devant same, et as après (jamais « same than » ni « same that »).

The same + nom + as : She has the same shoes as me.

Pour une ressemblance partielle : similar to (semblable à). Pour la différence : different from (en anglais britannique, on entend aussi different to).

  • Your bag is the same as mine!
    Identique.
  • We were born in the same year.
    the same + nom.
  • Belgian French is a little different from French in France.
    different from.

La formule

as + adjectif / adverbe + as · not as … as = moins … que · as much / as many + nom + as · the same (+ nom) as · similar to · different from

Prononciation

Pronunciation Lab

  • as est faible dans la phrase : /əz/. On dit « əz TALL əz », avec l'accent sur l'adjectif.
  • Dans as … as, les deux as sont faibles et liés : « as good as » ≈ /əz ˈɡʊd əz/.
  • Après not as, l'accent tombe sur l'adjectif : It isn't as EXpensive as the taxi.
  • the same /ðə ˈseɪm/ : le « a » se prononce /eɪ/, comme dans « name ».
  • twice /twaɪs/ se termine par un /s/ sourd, pas par /z/.

Les erreurs fréquentes

À éviterÀ direPourquoi
✗ She is as tall than her mother.✓ She is as tall as her mother.as … as, jamais as … than.
✗ This film is as more interesting as the first one.✓ This film is as interesting as the first one.L'adjectif reste à la forme simple.
✗ My phone is the same than yours.✓ My phone is the same as yours.the same as, jamais same than.
✗ We are in same class.✓ We are in the same class.Toujours the devant same.
✗ I have as many of friends as you.✓ I have as many friends as you.Pas de of après as many.
✗ He doesn't earn as many money as me.✓ He doesn't earn as much money as me.Money est indénombrable : much.
✗ She is different of her sister.✓ She is different from her sister.different from (ou to), pas of.
✗ My town is less big than Douala.✓ My town isn't as big as Douala.Less big est possible mais peu naturel.

En situation

Listening Studio

Deux colocataires à Ottawa comparent deux appartements avant de déménager.

Inès

So, what do you think? The flat on King Street or the one near the park?
Alors, qu'en penses-tu ? L'appartement de King Street ou celui près du parc ?

Samuel

The King Street flat isn't as big as the other one, but it's nearer the university.
Celui de King Street est moins grand que l'autre, mais il est plus près de l'université.

Inès

True. And the rent is the same as our rent now.
C'est vrai. Et le loyer est le même que notre loyer actuel.

Samuel

The park flat is nearly twice as expensive! We can't pay that much.
Celui du parc est presque deux fois plus cher ! On ne peut pas payer autant.

Inès

But it's as quiet as a village. I can sleep as long as I want!
Mais il est aussi calme qu'un village. Je peux dormir aussi longtemps que je veux !

Samuel

You don't sleep as much as you think, Inès.
Tu ne dors pas autant que tu le crois, Inès.

Inès

Ha! OK. The bedrooms in King Street are similar to ours, right?
Ha ! D'accord. Les chambres à King Street ressemblent aux nôtres, non ?

Samuel

Yes, and the kitchen is as modern as the one in the park flat.
Oui, et la cuisine est aussi moderne que celle de l'appartement du parc.

Inès

Then let's take King Street. It's not as nice, but it's cheaper.
Alors prenons King Street. Il est moins joli, mais il est moins cher.

Exercices

Writing Workshop

Du plus simple au plus difficile. Réponds sans regarder la leçon, puis vérifie chaque exercice.

1. As … as

Complète avec as … as et l'adjectif ou l'adverbe entre parenthèses.

  1. My sister is ___ my mother. (tall)
  2. This exercise is ___ the first one. (easy)
  3. He drives ___ a racing driver! (fast)
  4. Please answer ___ possible. (quickly)
  5. Is Yaoundé ___ Douala? (hot)
Voir le corrigé
  1. as tall as
  2. as easy as
  3. as fast as
  4. as quickly as
  5. as hot as

2. Not as … as

Réécris chaque phrase avec not as … as. Le sens doit rester le même.

  1. Paris is bigger than Brussels.
  2. The train is faster than the bus.
  3. My old job was more interesting than this one.
  4. Anna sings better than me.
Voir le corrigé
  1. Brussels isn't as big as Paris.
  2. The bus isn't as fast as the train.
  3. This job isn't as interesting as my old one.
  4. I don't sing as well as Anna.

3. Much, many ou same ?

Complète avec much, many, the same ou from.

  1. I don't have as ___ free time as last year.
  2. There weren't as ___ people as at the last concert.
  3. Her dress is ___ as mine!
  4. Life in Montréal is different ___ life in Dakar.
  5. You can eat as ___ cakes as you like.
Voir le corrigé
  1. much
  2. many
  3. the same
  4. from
  5. many

4. Corriger l'erreur

Chaque phrase contient une erreur. Corrige-la.

  1. He is as old than me.
  2. Our car is the same that yours.
  3. This book is as better as the film.
  4. We live in same street.
  5. I don't have as many money as you.
Voir le corrigé
  1. He is as old as me.
  2. Our car is the same as yours.
  3. This book is as good as the film.
  4. We live in the same street.
  5. I don't have as much money as you.

5. Traduire

Traduis en anglais.

  1. Mon frère est aussi grand que moi.
  2. Aujourd'hui, il fait moins froid qu'hier.
  3. Nous avons le même professeur.
  4. Viens dès que possible.
Voir le corrigé
  1. My brother is as tall as me. / My brother is as tall as I am.
  2. Today isn't as cold as yesterday.
  3. We have the same teacher.
  4. Come as soon as possible.

6. À toi

Compare-toi à une personne de ta famille ou à un ami. Écris quatre phrases : une avec as … as, une avec not as … as, une avec the same (as) et une avec as much / as many … as.

  1. Exemple de réponse
Voir le corrigé
  1. My cousin Paul is as old as me: we were both born in 2001. He isn't as tall as me, but he's much stronger. We have the same favourite team. He doesn't read as many books as I do.
À retenir
  • as + adjectif / adverbe (forme simple) + as = aussi … que.
  • not as … as = moins … que (plus naturel que less … than).
  • as much + indénombrable + as · as many + pluriel + as (sans of).
  • the same (+ nom) as · similar to · different from : jamais « same than ».
  • Après as : me / him (courant) ou I am / he is (soigné).
