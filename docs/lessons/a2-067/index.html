Les questions au passé (did…?)

Au présent, on pose une question avec do / does (leçon 6). Au passé simple, on utilise un seul auxiliaire pour toutes les personnes : did. Le grand piège pour les francophones : après did, le verbe revient à la base verbale. On dit « Did you go? », jamais « Did you went? ». Cette leçon revoit aussi la négation (didn't) et les questions au passé avec be (was / were).

⏱ 30–45 min✎ Rédigé par JG◎ Niveau A2

Envie d’immersion ? This lesson in English →

À la fin de cette leçon, tu sauras :
  • Former une question fermée au passé : Did + sujet + base verbale ?
  • Poser des questions ouvertes avec un mot interrogatif : Where did you go? What did she say?
  • Répondre brièvement : Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  • Ne pas utiliser did avec be : Were you tired? Was it good?

La question fermée : Did + sujet + base verbale

Pour poser une question au passé simple, on place did avant le sujet, puis on met le verbe à la base verbale (sans -ed, sans forme irrégulière) : You visited Rome. → Did you visit Rome?

Did est le même pour toutes les personnes : Did I…? Did he…? Did they…? Il n'y a pas de « dids » ni de forme spéciale à la 3e personne.

C'est did qui porte le passé. Le verbe principal ne doit donc plus être au passé : Did she buy (et non « bought ») a new phone?

AffirmationQuestionRéponse courte
You worked late.Did you work late?Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
She went to Douala.Did she go to Douala?Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
They saw the match.Did they see the match?Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.
It rained.Did it rain?Yes, it did. / No, it didn't.
  • Did you sleep well?
    sleep, pas slept.
  • Did Marc call you yesterday?
    call, pas called.
  • Did they enjoy the concert? — Yes, they did.
    Réponse courte avec did.

Les questions ouvertes : mot interrogatif + did

On ajoute le mot interrogatif (voir la leçon 29) au début : Wh- + did + sujet + base verbale ? Where did you go? What did you eat? When did they arrive?

L'ordre est le même avec How long, How much, What time… : How long did you stay? How much did it cost? What time did the film start?

Attention aux prépositions : en anglais, elles se placent souvent à la fin de la question. Who did you go with? (Avec qui es-tu allé ?) What did you talk about?

  • Where did you spend your holidays?
    Wh- + did + sujet + base.
  • What time did you get up this morning?
    What time + did.
  • Who did you meet at the party?
    Who = complément (la personne rencontrée).
  • Who did you travel with?
    Préposition à la fin.

La forme négative : didn't + base verbale

La négation suit la même logique : sujet + didn't (did not) + base verbale. I didn't see him. She didn't come.

Ne mets jamais le verbe au passé après didn't : « I didn't went » est faux.

Questions négatives pour exprimer la surprise : Didn't you like the film? (Tu n'as pas aimé le film ?)

  • I didn't understand the question.
    understand, pas understood.
  • We didn't have time to eat.
    have est aussi un verbe principal ici.
  • Didn't you get my message?
    Surprise.

Exception : be au passé (was / were)

Avec be, on n'utilise pas did. On inverse simplement le sujet et was / were (voir la leçon 39) : You were tired. → Were you tired? It was expensive. → Was it expensive?

Réponses courtes : Yes, I was. / No, it wasn't. Questions ouvertes : Where were you? How was your trip?

« How was…? » est très utilisé pour demander une impression : How was the weekend? How was the exam?

VerbeQuestionNégation
beWas she at home?She wasn't at home.
beWere they late?They weren't late.
autres verbesDid she stay at home?She didn't stay at home.
  • How was your trip to Montréal?
    Pas « How did your trip? ».
  • Were you at the meeting? — No, I wasn't.
    Réponse avec wasn't.

Did ou didn't ? Les réponses courtes

En réponse à une question avec did, on reprend did ou didn't, pas le verbe principal : Did you call her? — Yes, I did. (et non « Yes, I called. »).

Un simple « Yes. » ou « No. » est possible, mais « Yes, I did. » est plus poli et plus naturel.

Pour continuer la conversation, on ajoute souvent une information : No, I didn't. I was too busy.

  • Did you lock the door? — Yes, I did.
    Pas « Yes, I locked. ».
  • Did the children eat everything? — No, they didn't. They weren't hungry.
    Réponse + explication.

La formule

(Wh-) + did + sujet + base verbale ? · sujet + didn't + base verbale · Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. · be : Was / Were + sujet… ?

Prononciation

Pronunciation Lab

  • did se prononce /dɪd/ ; dans la question, il est souvent faible et lié au pronom : « Did you » ≈ /ˈdɪdʒu/ (« didjou »).
  • « Did he » se réduit souvent en /ˈdɪdi/ : le h disparaît à l'oral rapide.
  • didn't /ˈdɪdnt/ : deux syllabes, le t final est très léger.
  • Intonation : la question fermée (Did you…?) monte à la fin ↗ ; la question ouverte (Where did you…?) descend ↘.
  • Dans la réponse courte, did est accentué : Yes, I DID.

En situation

Listening Studio

Lundi matin, dans un bureau à Yaoundé, Grâce demande à son collègue Patrick comment s'est passé son week-end.

Grâce

Morning, Patrick! How was your weekend?
Bonjour, Patrick ! Comment s'est passé ton week-end ?

Patrick

Great, thanks. I went to Kribi with my brother.
Super, merci. Je suis allé à Kribi avec mon frère.

Grâce

Lucky you! How did you get there? Did you drive?
Quelle chance ! Comment y êtes-vous allés ? Tu as pris la voiture ?

Patrick

No, I didn't. We took the bus. It was cheaper.
Non. On a pris le bus. C'était moins cher.

Grâce

Where did you stay?
Où avez-vous logé ?

Patrick

In a small hotel near the beach. Did you do anything special?
Dans un petit hôtel près de la plage. Et toi, tu as fait quelque chose de spécial ?

Grâce

Not really. I stayed at home and watched a series.
Pas vraiment. Je suis restée à la maison et j'ai regardé une série.

Patrick

Which one? Was it good?
Laquelle ? C'était bien ?

Grâce

Yes, it was. But I didn't finish it. I fell asleep!
Oui. Mais je ne l'ai pas finie. Je me suis endormie !

Exercices

Writing Workshop

Du plus simple au plus difficile. Réponds sans regarder la leçon, puis vérifie chaque exercice.

1. Questions fermées

Transforme chaque phrase en question avec did.

  1. You watched the match.
  2. She bought a new car.
  3. They went to Liège.
  4. He forgot his keys.
  5. It snowed last night.
Voir le corrigé
  1. Did you watch the match?
  2. Did she buy a new car?
  3. Did they go to Liège?
  4. Did he forget his keys?
  5. Did it snow last night?

2. Questions ouvertes

Pose la question qui correspond à la partie en majuscules.

  1. I went to CASABLANCA.
  2. She arrived AT TEN O'CLOCK.
  3. They ate FISH AND RICE.
  4. The tickets cost 50 EUROS.
  5. We stayed THREE DAYS.
Voir le corrigé
  1. Where did you go?
  2. What time did she arrive? / When did she arrive?
  3. What did they eat?
  4. How much did the tickets cost?
  5. How long did you stay?

3. Did ou was / were ?

Complète avec Did, Was ou Were.

  1. ___ you at home yesterday evening?
  2. ___ your sister call you?
  3. ___ the film interesting?
  4. ___ they enjoy the meal?
  5. How ___ your exam?
Voir le corrigé
  1. Were
  2. Did
  3. Was
  4. Did
  5. was

4. Corriger l'erreur

Chaque phrase contient une erreur. Corrige-la.

  1. Did you saw my bag?
  2. What you did on Sunday?
  3. She didn't came to class.
  4. Did they were happy?
  5. Did you finish? — Yes, I finished.
Voir le corrigé
  1. Did you see my bag?
  2. What did you do on Sunday?
  3. She didn't come to class.
  4. Were they happy?
  5. Did you finish? — Yes, I did.

5. Traduire

Traduis en anglais.

  1. Tu as bien dormi ?
  2. Avec qui es-tu allé au cinéma ?
  3. Comment était le concert ?
  4. Elle n'a pas compris la question.
Voir le corrigé
  1. Did you sleep well?
  2. Who did you go to the cinema with?
  3. How was the concert?
  4. She didn't understand the question.

6. À toi

Écris cinq questions pour demander à un ami ce qu'il a fait le week-end dernier (une question fermée avec did, trois questions avec Where / What / Who, et une question avec How was…?). Puis réponds à deux d'entre elles.

  1. Exemple de réponse
Voir le corrigé
  1. Did you go out on Saturday? Where did you go? What did you do there? Who did you go with? How was it? — Yes, I did. I went to a concert with my cousin. It was fantastic!
À retenir
  • Question : (Wh-) + did + sujet + base verbale ? (Did you go? Where did you go?)
  • Négation : didn't + base verbale (I didn't see).
  • Après did / didn't, jamais de verbe au passé.
  • Réponses courtes : Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  • Avec be, pas de did : Were you…? Was it…? How was…?
