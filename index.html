THE CURRICULUM

Your learning path

Five levels. One coherent progression. Move forward at your own pace.

A1

Beginner

First interactions, essential vocabulary, simple sentences.

A2

Elementary

Everyday exchanges, past and future, practical communication.

B1

Intermediate

Independent communication, narratives, opinions and plans.

B2

Upper-intermediate

Nuance, argumentation, complex texts and fluent interaction.

C1

Advanced

Precision, flexibility, sophisticated expression and confidence.

How to use this path

Start with a level that feels comfortable. Work through the foundations, practise each skill, then return to review. The goal is durable understanding, not speed.

OUR APPROACH

Learn. Practise. Review. Repeat.

Language learning is not a race through a checklist. It is the gradual construction of understanding, confidence and independence.

01

Clarity before complexity

Understand one useful idea at a time. Connect new knowledge to what you already know.

02

Context makes it real

Examples and practice help you recognise when and how a structure is used.

03

Consistency over intensity

Short, regular sessions and active recall create lasting progress.
